# Commercial Automobile & Casualty Fleet Underwriting Guidelines
**Document ID:** CORP-UW-FLEET-2026-V1  
**Category:** Commercial Automobile, Fleet Operations, and Casualty Liability  
**Authority:** Commercial Lines Practice Leader / Actuarial Standards Board  

---

## 1. Commercial Auto Fleet Loss Experience
### [RULE-C-301] Fleet Loss Ratio & Severity Ceiling
- **Scope:** Commercial motor fleets, transit operations, and business auto policies.
- **Corporate Standard:** The maximum permissible **5-year cumulative loss ratio is 40%** of policy limits.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Accounts with loss ratios $\le 25\%$ qualify for standard fleet schedule credits.
  - Accounts with loss ratios between **25% and 40%**: Require loss control audit and mandatory fleet safety driver training program.
  - Accounts with loss ratios **exceeding 40%** or containing $\ge 2$ at-fault collision/liability incidents: Prohibited from standard binding authority. Referral to Senior Underwriting VP required, with a mandatory minimum collision/comprehensive deductible increase to **$5,000 per vehicle**.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Escalate to Senior Underwriter, double comprehensive/collision deductible to $5,000, and mandate fleet telematics installation.

## 2. Telematics & Driver Safety Governance
### [RULE-C-302] Telematics and Driver Monitoring Standards
- **Scope:** Commercial fleets comprising 5 or more powered commercial units.
- **Corporate Standard:** All commercial fleets with **5 or more vehicles** must utilize an approved continuous GPS telematics and Electronic Logging Device (ELD) solution.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Telematics system must track speeding events, harsh braking, and driver hours-of-service compliance.
  - Fleets lacking verified telematics systems will receive an automatic **15% fleet hazard surcharge** and a mandatory 60-day installation warranty.
  - Motor Vehicle Records (MVRs) for all commercial drivers must be pulled annually; any driver with $> 2$ moving violations or a major violation (DUI, reckless driving) must be excluded via named driver exclusion.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Form CA-99-10 (Telematics Compliance Warranty) and Named Driver Exclusion Endorsement.
