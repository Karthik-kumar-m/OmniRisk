# Natural Hazard, Flood, and Catastrophe Underwriting Guidelines
**Document ID:** CORP-UW-HAZ-2026-V1  
**Category:** Catastrophe Exposure, Environmental Hazards, and Flood Zoning  
**Authority:** Chief Risk Officer (CRO) / Catastrophe Modeling Committee  

---

## 1. Coastal Surge & Hurricane Exposure
### [RULE-H-201] Coastal Hurricane Exposure Threshold
- **Scope:** Properties situated in Atlantic, Gulf Coast, or coastal territories designated as Tier 1 Coastal Zones.
- **Corporate Standard:** Properties located within **5 miles of the coastline** or designated as **Tier 1 Coastal** are strictly barred from baseline all-peril deductible terms.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Standard all-peril deductible ($1,000–$2,500) CANNOT be applied to named windstorm/hurricane damage.
  - Underwriters must mandate a separate **Named Windstorm / Hurricane Percentage Deductible** of no less than **2% of the Total Insured Value (TIV)** (5% in High-Risk Surge Zones).
  - Standard property form must explicitly exclude coastal flood, storm surge, and wave wash unless backstopped by an authorized surplus lines treaty.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Form CP-10-38 (Mandatory Named Hurricane Percentage Deductible 2%–5%) and Flood Carve-Out Endorsement.

## 2. Flood Zoning & Hydrological Inundation
### [RULE-H-202] Flood Zone Restrictions
- **Scope:** FEMA Flood Zones and hydrological inundation models.
- **Corporate Standard:** Commercial property assets located in **FEMA Special Flood Hazard Areas (SFHA: Zones A, AE, AH, AO, V, VE)** require prior proof of primary flood insurance.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - The carrier will NOT provide primary flood coverage in SFHA designated flood zones.
  - The policyholder must maintain a separate active National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policy for the statutory maximum limits ($500,000 building / $500,000 contents).
  - Absence of certified NFIP policy proof constitutes a complete barrier to binding commercial property coverage.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Require certificate of active NFIP flood policy and attach water/flood exclusion endorsement.

## 3. Wildfire Urban Interface (WUI) Hazards
### [RULE-H-203] Wildfire Urban Interface Zone Standards
- **Scope:** Properties located in high-density vegetation zones, chaparral, or Wildfire Urban Interface zones.
- **Corporate Standard:** Properties with an **Environmental / Wildfire Hazard Score exceeding 70 out of 100** trigger mandatory defensible space verification.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Must provide photographic certification of at least **100 feet of continuous defensible brush clearance** surrounding all insured structures.
  - Attic vents must be retrofitted with ember-resistant mesh (1/8-inch or smaller).
  - Assets failing defensible clearance are ineligible for binding under preferred or standard tiers.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Mandate 100-ft defensible space warranty and Ember-Resistant Vent Endorsement.
