# Financial Stability, Moral Hazard, and Loss Threshold Guidelines
**Document ID:** CORP-UW-FIN-2026-V1  
**Category:** Financial Solvency, Moral Hazard, Prior History, and Claim Frequency  
**Authority:** Chief Actuary / Underwriting Audit Committee  

---

## 1. Historical Claim Frequency Standards
### [RULE-F-401] Claim Frequency Ceiling and Recurrent Perils
- **Scope:** All lines of business (Homeowners, Commercial Property, Commercial Auto, General Liability).
- **Corporate Standard:** The maximum allowable annual claim frequency is **0.40 claims per year** (or no more than 2 claims in a 5-year observation window).
- **Rule Specification:**
  - **Zero claims in 5 years (0.0/yr)**: Qualifies for Preferred Tier (Tier 1) rating with maximum premium credits.
  - **1 to 2 claims in 5 years ($\le 0.40$/yr)**: Qualifies for Standard Tier (Tier 2); requires review of loss causes.
  - **3 or more claims in 5 years ($> 0.40$/yr)**: Constitutes an unacceptable frequency hazard. The underwriter CANNOT offer preferred terms. Account must be classified as Moderate Risk (Tier 3) or Declinable (Tier 4).
  - **Recurrent Peril Rule**: If 2 or more claims share the same cause of loss (e.g. repeated Water damage, repeated Collision), the policy must impose a targeted peril deductible or specific peril carve-out.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Increase deductible by at least 50% across affected peril and require loss mitigation inspection.

## 2. Prior Carrier Non-Renewal & Involuntary Cancellation
### [RULE-F-402] Involuntary Cancellation / Non-Renewal Governance
- **Scope:** Moral hazard, premium non-payment, fraud indicators, and unmitigated underwriting recommendations.
- **Corporate Standard:** Any applicant subjected to **cancellation or non-renewal by a prior insurance carrier within the preceding 36 months** represents severe adverse selection.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Standard binding authority is strictly REVOKED.
  - Mandatory written disclosure from prior insurer stating reason for cancellation (e.g. loss frequency, failure to comply with recommendations, fraud, non-payment).
  - If cancellation was for non-compliance with inspection repairs, binding is PROHIBITED until an independent loss control inspection verifies 100% remediation.
  - If cancellation was for loss history, referral to Senior Underwriter is mandatory.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Escalate to Senior Underwriter; execute prior loss warranty statement; require certified loss control inspection prior to quote.

## 3. Financial Stability & Credit Tier Benchmarks
### [RULE-F-403] Financial Stability and Credit Tier Guidelines
- **Scope:** Insurance scoring, commercial credit rating, and financial solvency.
- **Corporate Standard:** Minimum acceptable insurance credit score for standard terms is **620 (Fair Tier or higher)**.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Scores **750 to 850 (Excellent)**: Eligible for maximum financial stability tier discount.
  - Scores **670 to 749 (Good)**: Standard eligibility.
  - Scores **580 to 669 (Fair)**: Standard terms; loss control inspection recommended.
  - Scores **below 580 (Poor / Sub-Prime)**: High correlation with adverse loss reporting and moral hazard. The policyholder CANNOT be granted monthly or quarterly installment billing. Full **100% upfront annual premium payment** or electronic funds transfer (EFT) escrow is required.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Enforce full annual premium prepayment warranty and attach restricted cancellation terms for non-payment.
