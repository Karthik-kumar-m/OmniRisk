# Commercial Property Underwriting Guidelines Manual
**Document ID:** CORP-UW-PROP-2026-V1  
**Category:** Commercial Property & Physical Asset Standards  
**Authority:** Chief Underwriting Officer (CUO) / CPCU Governance Board  

---

## 1. Roof Age and Structural Condition Standards
### [RULE-P-101] Roof Age Limitation
- **Scope:** All commercial structures, residential complexes, and habitational risks.
- **Corporate Standard:** The maximum permissible age of the primary roof covering is **20 years**.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Roofs aged **0 to 15 years**: Qualify for standard replacement cost valuation (RCV) with baseline deductibles.
  - Roofs aged **16 to 19 years**: Require submission of a licensed structural engineer roof inspection report completed within the last 12 months.
  - Roofs aged **20 years or older**: Automatic guideline breach. The policy CANNOT be bound under standard RCV terms. Mandatory Actual Cash Value (ACV) roof schedule endorsement or minimum 25% roof depreciation schedule must be applied.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Impose mandatory roof inspection warranty or endorse with Form CP-04-18 (Roof ACV Carve-Out).

## 2. Construction Classification & Material Resistance
### [RULE-P-102] Construction Class Standards
- **Scope:** Building material fire resistance and load-bearing classification.
- **Corporate Standard:** Standard underwriting authority extends to **ISO Class 4 (Masonry Non-Combustible)**, **ISO Class 5 (Modified Fire Resistive)**, and **ISO Class 6 (Fire Resistive)**.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Structures classified as **ISO Class 1 (Frame)** or **ISO Class 2 (Joisted Masonry)** with square footage exceeding 20,000 sq ft violate standard property thresholds.
  - Binding requires certification of a functioning automatic fire sprinkler system (NFPA 13 compliant) with central station telemetry.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Require NFPA 13 annual inspection certificate and 100% sprinkler warranty.

---

## 3. Public Fire Protection & Hydrant Proximity
### [RULE-P-103] Fire Protection Class Standards
- **Scope:** Municipal fire protection class ratings (ISO Protection Class 1 through 10).
- **Corporate Standard:** Favorable underwriting eligibility applies to properties within **ISO Protection Classes 1 through 6**.
- **Rule Specification:**
  - Properties situated in **Protection Classes 7, 8, 9, or 10** (typically rural or unprotected areas lacking pressurized municipal hydrants within 1,000 feet) trigger an underwriting referral.
  - Mandatory on-site secondary water supply (minimum 10,000 gallon tank) or verified local volunteer fire company mutual aid agreement required.
- **Actionable Mitigation:** Impose higher all-other-peril (AOP) deductible of at least $5,000 and verified on-site water source.
